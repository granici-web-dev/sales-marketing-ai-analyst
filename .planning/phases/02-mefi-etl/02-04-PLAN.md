<objective>
Create the `backfill_mefi_leads` Celery task that runs once after the first sync (auto-triggered by `sync_mefi_leads` when `raw_mefi_leads` count is 0) and fills 12 months of historical lead data using chunked month-by-month pagination on the `backfill` Celery queue.
